FAQ

Questions sellers usually ask

I don't have a logo or brand yet. Can you still build my site?

Yes. Most first-time sellers start this way. We'll build a simple, clean look from your product photos and colors, and you can develop a full brand later.

Can I update the site myself after launch?

Yes. Every site is built so you can edit text, photos, and products without touching code. You'll get a short walkthrough at launch.

Do I need to already have a domain name?

No. If you don't have one, we'll pick and set one up together as part of the project.

How much does a site actually cost?

It depends on the size of the site and how much ongoing management you want, from a small static site to full hands-off upkeep. Message me with what you're picturing and I'll send a real number within a day.

What if I need something more custom?

Message me and describe the project. Larger catalogs, bookings, memberships, or multi-brand sites can all be quoted directly.
